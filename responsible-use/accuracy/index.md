# Accuracy

Scientific and medical visualization depends on rigor and accuracy. Generative systems can produce plausible-looking images that contain factual errors, including errors in anatomy, cells, or molecular biology. Language and image models can also present hallucinations confidently.

Treat AI output as material that requires expert review. Verify claims and visual details against reliable references, and ensure a knowledgeable person is accountable for the final communication. In high-stakes work, consider whether the risk of a misleading output outweighs the practical benefit of using AI.
